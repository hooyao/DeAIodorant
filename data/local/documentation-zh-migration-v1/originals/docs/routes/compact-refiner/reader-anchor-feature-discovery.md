   more than five recurring linguistic families that may explain the intensity
   contrast. The analyst knows the human labels; this is label-conditioned
   hypothesis discovery, not blind confirmation. Preserve necessary contrasts,
   useful direct advice, concrete detail, and other counterexamples.
2. A separate claim/relation analyst reads SMZDM in full and checks internal
   scope, reference, and inferential continuity, including consistency with
   the publisher's embedded quote snippets. Distinguish missing qualification,
   uncertain reference, unsupported extrapolation, and actual contradiction.
   Do not certify technical truth from an unverified quote or invent a premise.

Each proposed family or defect requires exact evidence, Unicode offsets and
block IDs, an explanation in context, counterexamples/alternative explanations,
and a statement of whether cheap NLP can observe it directly or merely nominate
it for semantic inspection. Avoid an opaque composite smell score, a long
post-hoc feature search, and a subject-verb-object presence rule.

Keep surface aversion separate from content/reading damage. Frequent brisk
advice, compressed parallel slogans, and interpersonal stance might irritate
without being grammatically wrong. A claim can also lose a crucial technical
qualification while remaining fluent. Neither path is reducible to a list of
forbidden words. Ordinary Chinese ellipsis and useful category restrictions
remain valid counterexamples.
