# Target-Coverage Rescreen After the Cowork Sample Rejection

Protocol: `target-coverage-rescreen-1.0`. Date: 2026-09-14.
Purpose: correct sample selection before another rewrite or reader request.
This is exploratory nomination, not a smell classifier or efficacy study.

## Selection failure being corrected

The maintainer already described Cowork as weak in AI smell. The assistant
nevertheless repeated work and a reader request because the body was available,
contained grammar defects, and could support a preservation-reviewed repair.
That substituted generic repairability for coverage of the intended phenomenon.
Including translation in the product scope did not justify this substitution.

The maintainer further explains that Cowork may inherit English LLM problems
through translation while still failing to exemplify strongly characteristic
Chinese AI-style writing. Translation provenance is explanatory context, not a
binary severity label. Do not conclude that all translations lack the target,
that all direct-Chinese writing has it, or that native-Chinese authorship is
proved by Chinese names, topics, or lack of translation metadata.

Cowork reader preview v1 is withdrawn for sample mismatch. Preserve its inputs,
revisions, semantic reviews, rendering work, and the maintainer's exact feedback.
No A/B preference, no-improvement outcome, or semantic-preservation judgment was
given by this rejection. Do not reissue the same task under a readability label.

## Bounded rescreen

Read all twelve complete post-period bodies in the already acquired
`data/local/media-provenance-extension-v1/`. This frame is chosen for recency
and availability and contains both zeros and nonzeros of the nominated marker;
do not rank/filter by the count. These are exposed development documents,
not fresh validation. No new bodies, rewrite variants, or user tasks are
generated by this rescreen.

For each article, record:

- its format and whether it matches a complete informative media article;
- source/translation/quotation evidence and what remains unknown;
- whether there are sustained clusters of the nominated problems, with exact
  source evidence and paragraph locations: recurring unnecessary opposition,
  repeated abstract conclusions, argument/reference discontinuity, and
  unconnected progression;
- necessary contrasts, coherent exposition, quotation/genre explanations, and
  other counterexamples;
- whether the only defects are generic translationese, isolated typos,
  extraction artifacts, jargon, promotional tone, or unsupported facts;
- a nomination disposition: `sustained_target_candidate`, `weak_or_ambiguous`,
  or `poor_format_fit`, with uncertainty and a concrete reason.

There is no requirement to nominate a candidate. Factual unsupportedness or one
ungrammatical sentence alone cannot make an article a strong-style example.
A high marker count in a long interview is not sufficient. A repeated maneuver
needs identifiable counterparts in the whole article, and an ordinary
interview recap or technical distinction may remain justified.

These dispositions describe assistant screening, not the maintainer's felt
severity, authorship, origin, or a training label. Preserve any failure to find
an adequately contrasted case instead of filling a reader quota. Do not send
the new candidates to the maintainer merely because this screen has completed.
