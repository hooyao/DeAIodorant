# Hypothesis: Recurrent Corrective Framing Without Sufficient Explanatory Work

Version: `rhetorical-mechanism-hypothesis-0.1`. Date: 2026-09-14.
Status: proposed behavioral explanation, not an established training cause.

## Proposed underlying rhetorical action

A recurrent discourse move supplies an apparently shallow interpretation or
classification, rejects it, and offers an apparently deeper account. It can
produce the impression of correcting a misconception or revealing an essence
without supplying corresponding new evidence, conditions, or reasoning.

The hypothesis is about repeated reliance on this move, especially where the
initial foil was never motivated, the two descriptions concern different
dimensions, or the conclusion is only a broader label. The move can survive
many surface forms: a bare copula, appearance/reality framing, an asserted real
key or essence, or a presupposed reader-agenda instruction.

Three candidate failures should be distinguished:

1. **Unmotivated opposition:** a rejected alternative is introduced without
   showing that it is relevant, plausible, or incompatible with the proposed
   account. This need not be a formal logical contradiction.
2. **Renaming in place of explanation:** a concrete object or activity acquires
   a higher-level category or slogan without clarifying its mechanisms,
   conditions, tradeoffs, or implications.
3. **Repeated verdicts in place of progression:** successive passages each
   announce a correction or a conclusion, but the relationships among those
   conclusions remain underspecified. Readers must reconstruct those bridges.

The title's `先把……算明白` frame can express a related interpersonal stance:
the narrator presupposes that it has organized the reader's problem and will
supply the decisive interpretation. Enumerating practical choices can be useful;
the possible aversion concerns recurrent prepackaged authority and compressed
judgments, not the presence of a useful outline alone.

## Why a generator might favor it

This is a familiar, reusable rhetorical pattern with a predictable continuation.
It fits instructions to be analytical, helpful, decisive, or insightful and can
organize a paragraph with little newly established reasoning. Learned writing
conventions, instruction examples, synthetic-data reuse, or preference
optimization could reinforce it. These are competing possibilities. Current
article observations do not isolate RLHF, DPO, any reward model, English-first
reasoning, or proprietary training-data composition.

Do not claim conscious deception or an inspected internal planning mechanism.
The current evidence supports describing the output's rhetorical behavior and
testing whether that behavior contributes to the reader's response.

## Counterexamples and measurement requirements

A Jetson/RK3566 correction distinguishes actual hardware alternatives. Software
openness versus unpublished hardware plans is a meaningful restriction. Some
summaries and useful consumer advice legitimately adopt decisive phrasing.
Necessary negation and distinctions must remain in refinement outputs.

Candidate measurement therefore needs context: the foil and affirmative claim,
their semantic relationship, prior supporting evidence, new information added,
and recurrence of the same move. Surface counts can locate candidates but
cannot determine whether a correction is motivated or an explanation adequate.

Two prospective invariance checks follow from the maintainer's clarification:

- A connective-only paraphrase that preserves the same rhetorical action should
  not substantially improve the eventual diagnostic merely by changing tokens.
- A revision that actually clarifies a relationship or removes an unsupported
  rhetorical maneuver should be evaluated for reader benefit and preservation,
  even if it retains a contrast marker.

These checks are requirements for a future measure, not executed intervention
results. No new rewrite, score, model training, or user rating was produced by
this hypothesis record.
