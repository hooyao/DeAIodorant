## Candidate style families

The style analyst read both complete articles and knew the supplied labels.
This is label-conditioned explanation, not blind confirmation. Four families
were retained; their selected examples overlap and are not exhaustive counts.

| Family | Observable form | Contrast and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Contrastive redefinition | Rejected/secondary category followed or preceded by an affirmative category, including implicit and reversed forms | Present in both articles; weak standalone explanation of the intensity difference |
| Short parallel verdicts | Repeated balanced clauses at headings, paragraph endings, and conclusions | More continuously prominent in the strong sample; can also aid useful scanning |
| Insider stance and reader direction | Community familiarity, assumed shared experience, audience labels, imperatives, prohibitions, and closing challenges | The strongest qualitative contrast together with parallel verdicts; buyer-guide genre is a major confounder |
| Evidence-to-verdict pivots | Bundles of figures, names, and observations followed by a broad explanation or recommendation | Common in both articles; semantic support must be checked separately |

A recurring compact tradeoff such as `买的是管线，熬的是时间` illustrates the
parallel-verdict hypothesis. The repeated informed-friend stance and instructions
make this a proposed interaction among cadence, interpersonal stance, and
judgment. An individual imperative, colloquial phrase, or parallel clause is
not automatically a defect.

The independent analysis preserves necessary hardware/software distinctions,
concrete platform specifications, delivery qualifications, uncertainty about
sales verification, and useful conditional advice. Strong smell does not imply
that the article has no useful information or that every sentence is wrong.

## Underlying reading and reasoning problems

A separate analyst read all 30 blocks and all 22 publisher quote snippets,
without reading the style analyst's output. Four principal findings were kept:

1. A quoted individual retail-parts estimate is promoted into a general
   no-savings verdict, then reused to infer commercial reverse-engineering
   pricing. Reused parts, scope of the build, and production scale are not held
   constant. This is a missing bridge and change of scope, not a verified
   numerical contradiction.
2. The advice to align a version `三件套` has no uniquely identified set of
   three components. Runtime and policy versions are mentioned, but the reader
   must guess the intended set before following the instruction.
3. Another product's ecosystem and early simulation examples do not establish
   the article's first-to-market or no-substitute claims. The comparison class
   and evidence required for exclusivity are missing from the inspected text.
4. The adjacent sales-rate statement and revenue-derived unit count lack a
   shared time window and accounting basis. Different windows or definitions
   could explain them; no factual contradiction is certified.

These checks localize where the article asks the reader to accept or supply a
relation that has not been established. They do not prove that those defects
cause the reported aversion or that every cited source is wrong.

The compact compute contrast at b006 deserves particular restraint. In context
it can mean that the robot does not need training-class GPU resources. It must
not be automatically interpreted as a literal denial of all onboard computing,
or as a missing-subject grammatical error. Root's initial observation about
compressed qualification remains a style/precision hypothesis, not a confirmed
technical mistake.

## Implications for traditional NLP

Cheap observable candidates include copular contrast patterns, adjacent clause
parallelism, paragraph-final evaluative formulations, pronouns and imperative
forms with speaker context, and quantified references whose antecedents need
inspection. Preserve DOM roles and keep quoted speech/citation markers separate.

Their counts cannot determine whether a contrast is necessary, expertise is
earned, advice is appropriate, or a conclusion follows. Source support and
reference resolution need contextual assessment. The new families have not
been converted into a scalar quality score, removal rule, or RL reward.

The next study should test whether this combination recurs in independent real
articles, with comparable buyer-guide/DIY controls so ordinary genre conventions
are not mistaken for the target. Use the supplied strong anchor to localize
features; do not replace the target again with generic typos or an easy weak
sample. Translation refinement remains a required product capability.
