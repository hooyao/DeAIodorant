# Pre/Post Counts of the Contrastive Replacement Family

Date: 2026-09-14. Protocol: `ershi-cohort-statistics-1.0`.
Status: descriptive available-corpus statistics, not authorship classification,
reader-effect validation, or a ready-to-use reward.

## Result

The literal `而是` occurs much more frequently in the post-period InfoQ sample.
The difference appears in both the earlier pilot and the new seeded sitemap
sample. The result concerns the reader's nominated observable cue; it does not
establish that every occurrence is irritating, unnecessary, or generated by AI.

Primary display below excludes known deterministic translations and the
historically documented translated pilot item. Retained articles are not thereby
certified original or high quality; further provenance and format review remains.

| Quantity | Before 2023-01-01 | On/after 2025-07-01 |
|---|---:|---:|
| InfoQ articles | 17 | 18 |
| CJK characters | 48,290 | 60,406 |
| Literal `而是` occurrences | 5 | 52 |
| Occurrences per 10,000 CJK characters | 1.04 | 8.61 |
| Articles containing at least one occurrence | 4 / 17 (23.5%) | 9 / 18 (50.0%) |
| Median occurrence count across all articles | 0 | 0.5 |
| Median count among articles containing the cue | 1 | 5 |

The pooled character-normalized frequency ratio is approximately 8.3. The
positive-document medians also distinguish occasional use from repetition.
Neither statistic is a calibrated threshold for perceived smell.

## Lexical variants

All literal occurrences count even when the left prefix cannot be assigned by
the fixed lexical catalog. The following family grouping is secondary and
heuristic; it does not establish semantic opposition or grammatical correctness.

| Left-prefix family followed by `而是` | Pre | Post |
|---|---:|---:|
| `不是` / `并不是` | 1 | 18 |
| `并非` | 0 | 7 |
| `不只是` / `不仅是` / `并不只是` | 0 | 7 |
| `不再是` | 1 | 4 |
| `不再只是` | 0 | 2 |
| `并不认为` | 0 | 1 |
| No catalog prefix matched in the permitted window | 3 | 13 |
| Total | 5 | 52 |

The unmatched cases are retained, not discarded or labeled as syntax errors.
They can involve other negation forms, cross-sentence relations, quotation,
or a genuinely different use. The data do not justify silently extending a
frozen lexicon to force them into desired categories.

The current Cowork article contains eight literal occurrences: four `并非`,
two `不再只是`, one `并不是`, and one `并不认为` pairings. It also contains
four `而不是` expressions, counted separately and not included in the eight.
The reader still described this article as weak in AI smell but difficult and
translationese-like, so eight occurrences do not become an intensity label.

## Separate acquisition batches

Known-translation exclusions applied; all nonempty body lengths included:

| Batch | Pre articles | Pre occurrences / per 10,000 CJK | Post articles | Post occurrences / per 10,000 CJK |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Existing diagnostic pilot | 10 | 1 / 0.44 | 9 | 22 / 7.10 |
| New seeded sitemap staging | 7 | 4 / 1.56 | 9 | 30 / 10.19 |

The shared direction is useful as a hypothesis signal. The batches are small
and selected differently: the pilot favored available visibility, while the new
batch uses fixed seeded sitemap URLs. They are not a representative random
sample of Chinese media or a completed source/topic/format match.

## Sensitivity and concentration

| View | Pre articles | Pre per 10,000 CJK | Post articles | Post per 10,000 CJK |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| All nonempty InfoQ bodies, including known translations | 18 | 0.96 | 22 | 8.38 |
| Known translations excluded | 17 | 1.04 | 18 | 8.61 |
| Known translations excluded, at least 1000 CJK characters | 13 | 1.10 | 13 | 9.00 |

The 1000-CJK restriction is a prespecified short-format sensitivity, not a
quality gate. It excludes some short FAQs, news items, and announcements while
retaining all other evidence in the raw outputs.

One post-period article contributes 19 of the 52 occurrences and has unresolved
provenance. A post-hoc concentration check removes that one top contributor
without changing any feature definition: the remaining post sample has 33
occurrences, 7.70 per 10,000 CJK, and a rate ratio of about 7.4 relative to pre.
This shows that the aggregate difference is not solely that article. It does
not validate exclusion by style or eliminate other confounding.

The pre count distribution is 13 zeros, three ones, and one two. The post
distribution is nine zeros, two ones, one two, one three, two fives, two eights,
and one nineteen. Half of the retained post articles contain no `而是` at all.
This cue is therefore not a universal property of post-period writing.

## Scope and limitations

Main comparison: InfoQ only. The ten pre-period Machine Heart pilot articles are
measured separately in the machine-readable output, never pooled into the
InfoQ temporal comparison. The transition interval is excluded.

Bodies retain quotations, code/example text, and body headings as captured;
titles are separate (zero title occurrences in these primary InfoQ groups).
Counts do not distinguish a journalist's original wording from quoted material.
The lexical pairing window tolerates extractor-inserted newlines but may still
pair unrelated clauses. All literal and classified instances have audit offsets.

Known translation exclusion uses existing evidence, not a claim that every
remaining item passes formal corpus admission. Body completeness, updates to old
pages, topical change, publication format, translationese, author/source mix,
and visibility-age bias remain relevant. No claim is made that publication
date proves individual authorship or that July 2025 is an empirically localized
causal breakpoint.

No confirmatory p-value or feature-promotion decision is attached. This narrow
reader-nominated feature gives a concrete descriptive result; earlier weak
sample limitations do not make such a count impossible. A future experiment
must establish which repeated constructions increase reading difficulty and
which are necessary distinctions, before any removal rule or reward is used.

## Product interpretation

The maintainer's two targets remain separate: reduce aversive repetitive cues
and repair actual reading defects. This run measures only the first cue family.
It does not count missing predicate arguments, diagnose scattered logic, or
certify readability. In particular, a restriction such as limited versus full
file authorization may require a contrast even when other contrasts are
ornamental. Deleting all `而是` occurrences would be an invalid product rule.

## Reproduction

```powershell
python experiments/ershi_cohort_statistics.py --output-dir feature_runs/ershi-cohort-v1
python -m pytest tests/test_reading_burden.py
```

The runner refuses existing output directories; use a new directory to reproduce
and compare the deterministic counts. It validates all source hashes, excludes
empty captures, deduplicates IDs/URLs/normalized bodies, and verifies that the
helper's literal count equals an independent string count for every document.

Private outputs: `feature_runs/ershi-cohort-v1/summary.json`,
`document_counts.csv`, `document_counts.jsonl`, `instances.jsonl`, and
`manifest.json`. The manifest records source/implementation/protocol hashes,
Python version, the actual command, and output hashes. Existing corpus bodies,
annotations, and `configs/features.v1.json` are unchanged. No LLM inference,
syntax-model run, GPU, or new collection was needed for this statistic.
